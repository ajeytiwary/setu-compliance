# JSW Steel Vijayanagar → EU: public-record EuroSetu case study

## Scope
This is an evidence-backed reconstruction, **not** a claim that EuroSetu is deployed by JSW Steel. It uses public records to populate what can be substantiated and leaves confidential fields incomplete.

## Publicly substantiated facts
- FY2025-26 JSW group export sales were ~2.80 MnT; Europe represented 66.8%. JSW Steel exports included 1.13 MnT HRC.
- Vijayanagar is JSW's largest single-location steel facility.
- A valid International EPD System declaration (EPD-IES-0005172:001, version 13 Mar 2026, valid to 12 Mar 2031) covers Hot Rolled Coils from Vijayanagar.
- The EPD describes BF+BOF production, continuous casting to slab and hot rolling to coil.
- The EPD A1-A3 GWP-GHG result is an LCA/EPD result, **not a CBAM embedded-emissions value**.
- JSW's FY2025-26 reporting says it has plant-wise emissions accounting aligned with EU methodologies and engaged EU-ETS-accredited third-party verifiers.

## Missing/private data required
Actual EU sales order/invoice/importer, exact shipment CN, coil/heat/slab genealogy, CBAM installation boundaries and activity data, precursor emissions, accredited verification statement, supplier declarations, shipment-specific recycled content and final steel-DPP fields.

## Why the MVP matters
The product connects the private plant-level records behind public disclosures and compiles them into an auditable shipment-level market-access package rather than inventing sustainability data.
