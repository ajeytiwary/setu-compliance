# Setu public-record case library

The MVP carries ten candidate enterprise cases: five steel producers and five expansion sectors. These are public-evidence-backed targets for a data-access pilot.

## Steel
1. JSW Steel — Vijayanagar HRC (anchor case)
2. Tata Steel — India flat/value-added steel
3. SAIL — HRC/plate/CR/galvanised/long products
4. AM/NS India — Hazira flat steel
5. Jindal Stainless — stainless coil/sheet/plate/wire rod

## Next priority products

7. Arvind — textiles/apparel
8. Apollo Tyres — tyres
9. Godrej Interio — furniture
10. Amber Enterprises — electronics/PCB/PCBA
