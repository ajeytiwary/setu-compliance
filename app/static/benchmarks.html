What these labels mean

NORMATIVE

Reference-checked

Reserved for tests whose expected result is derived directly from a named authoritative source artifact. Internal examples and controlled TARIC fixtures are explicitly not labelled NORMATIVE.

EXTERNAL-ORACLE

Captured comparison

Compared with a captured third-party tool output stored as a fixture — never live scraping. Proves agreement on listed cases; the oracle is not a certification authority.

COMPETITIVE-SCENARIO / SYNTHETIC

Workflow proof

Competitor-inspired workflow scenarios run on synthetic data. Proves the product behaviour (blocking, lineage, reconciliation); commercial values are illustrative, not customer results.

No competitor logos are used. No parity, replacement, or certification claim is made. Aspirational targets are never published as achieved metrics.
