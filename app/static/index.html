THE PROBLEM

Compliance is fragmented. Revenue is not.

ERP knows the order. MES knows production. Sustainability owns emissions. Procurement chases suppliers. Customs handles classification and documents. Regulations change independently. EuroSetu creates one transaction level control plane across them.

THE CONTROL PLANE

One shipment. Every market-access requirement.

01

Compile obligations

Start with product, CN code, origin, destination, facility, customer and date. Determine which rule families and evidence requirements apply.

02

Trace evidence

Connect claims to source records, suppliers, plant data, methodologies and verification. Missing information stays visible instead of silently passing.

03

Prioritise by money

Move from “documents missing” to “this evidence gap blocks these orders and this much export value.” Focus remediation where it protects the most value.
