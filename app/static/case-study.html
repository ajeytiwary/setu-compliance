CASE STUDY · SYNTHETIC COMMERCIAL DATA

One steel shipment, an explainable blocker queue.

This example uses a synthetic India→EU hot-rolled-coil shipment. The €340,000 value and shipment identity are illustrative, not a customer result. Regulatory inputs are versioned separately.

The shipment

Commercial line

Order SYN-PO-HRC-001, line L1: 200 tonnes of hot-rolled coil, CN 7208 39 00, from India to the Netherlands. The €340,000 value is synthetic.

Blocked decision

Missing or unverified evidence is shown with its requirement and source. The workflow blocks release while those gaps remain.

Remediation and replay

New evidence creates a new decision linked to the previous one. Reviewers can compare the causal change and replay the audit trail.

READY means the demonstrated rule and evidence gates pass on pinned inputs. It does not mean customs acceptance, verifier sign-off, or CBAM registry submission.
