TRUST & SECURITY

Evidence infrastructure should make its security boundary explicit.

EuroSetu is being hardened for customer-authorised ERP, MES, EMS, supplier and verifier data. This page states implemented controls and deliberately does not claim certifications we do not hold.

Implemented in the product

Identity boundary

Privileged pilot APIs require a signed bearer token. Tenant membership and roles come from signed claims and cannot be asserted with free-form role headers.

Least privilege

Marketing demo access is separate from pilot roles. Evidence verification and resolution require verifier/admin authority; contributor operations use separate roles.

Evidence integrity

Evidence and regulatory payloads use SHA-256 provenance. Regulatory decisions retain source/version metadata and fail closed when required information is unavailable.

Production deployment requirements

Customer deployments require an approved identity provider, database-enforced tenant isolation, encrypted transport and storage, secrets management, backup/restore validation, retention/deletion policy, audit logging and customer-specific access review. These controls must be verified in the deployment; this public page is not a certification statement.

DATA BOUNDARY

Demo: synthetic commercial rows + versioned public regulatory sources.

Pilot: customer-authorised scoped extracts only.

Production: tenant-scoped identity, storage and audit controls required.
